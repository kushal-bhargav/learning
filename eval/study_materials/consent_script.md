# Consent Script

Study title: Gift Intelligence Agency Console Evaluation

Facilitator script:

Thank you for taking part in this study. We are evaluating a research prototype called GMGI, or Gift Intelligence, which helps people create personalized gift concepts using a mix of human choices and AI-generated suggestions.

The purpose of this study is to understand how different interaction styles affect your sense of authorship, control, satisfaction, novelty, and trust while creating a gift. We are not evaluating you or your creativity. We are evaluating the system design.

You will complete three short gift-creation sessions. In each session, you will use a different version of the system:

1. An AI-autonomous condition, where the system makes the creative decisions.
2. A human-only baseline, where you make choices manually without AI generation.
3. A negotiated hybrid condition, where you can accept, edit, regenerate, or delegate AI suggestions.

After each session, you will answer a brief questionnaire with 5-point rating questions and one open-ended question about who or what made the creative decisions.

The study should take about 20 to 30 minutes. You may stop at any time without penalty. You may skip any question you do not want to answer.

We will collect your questionnaire responses and interaction logs from the study interface, including counts of actions such as accept, edit, regenerate, and delegate. Please do not enter private, sensitive, or identifying personal information into the prototype. The study uses synthetic personas and pre-seeded demo memories.

The main risks are minimal: you may find the interface awkward, repetitive, or mildly frustrating. There is no direct benefit to you, but your feedback may help improve tools for human-AI creative collaboration.

Your responses will be analyzed in aggregate. Open-ended responses may be quoted in research materials only after removing identifying information.

Do you have any questions before we begin?

Consent check:

- I understand the study purpose and procedure.
- I understand that participation is voluntary.
- I understand that I may stop at any time.
- I agree not to enter private or sensitive personal information.
- I agree to participate in this study.

Record consent before starting the first condition.
